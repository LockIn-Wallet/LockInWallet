# Referral Program — Incentives Design

Status: **design proposal** (nothing here is implemented yet). On-chain referral
*recording* is live: every user who locks in their wallet through a
`?ref=<address>` link has their referrer and signup timestamp permanently
stored in the EVM `ReferralModule` and surfaced via the `ReferralRecorded`
event. This document designs what we can offer referrers — especially crypto
influencers — on top of that record.

## 1. Principles

- **Record first, reward later.** All reward schemes below are computable
  retroactively from on-chain data (`ReferralRecorded` + deposit/withdrawal/
  penalty events), so we can launch recording now and attach any incentive
  later without asking users to re-register.
- **Pay only for verified activity.** No reward is owed for a bare signup;
  rewards key off funded, retained wallets (see Activation).
- **Pull, not push.** Payouts are claim-based (referrer calls `claim()`),
  never automatic transfers — reviewable, pausable, and gas is on the claimer.
- **One referrer forever.** The referrer is immutable after lock-in (enforced
  on-chain), which kills referrer-churn attacks and makes attribution simple.

## 2. Revenue sources available for sharing

The product has no subscription and takes no APR commission. Today's actual
money flows:

| Source | Amount | Sink |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit-proxy deployment fee | 3 USDT per permanent deposit address | `ProxyDeploymentModule.treasuryAddress` |
| Early-withdrawal penalties (personal vaults) | `penaltyRateBps` of the withdrawn amount (default 20%) | `VaultSystemModule.treasury` |
| Future protocol token / points | n/a | retroactive distribution |

Community-vault penalties are redistributed to vault members and are **not**
available for referral revshare.

## 3. Activation definition (anti-sybil cornerstone)

A referred signup **counts as activated** only when all of:

1. Setup committed (wallet locked in) — already recorded on-chain;
2. Minimum net deposit, e.g. **≥ 25 USDT equivalent**;
3. Retention: the deposit is held **≥ 30 days** (net of withdrawals).

Everything below pays on *activated* referrals only. This makes wash-signups
(creating wallets you refer yourself from fresh addresses) cost more in
deposits-at-risk, time, and gas than the reward is worth.

## 4. Offer menu for crypto influencers

Composable — start with (a) + (d), add revshare tiers as volume proves out.

### a) Activation bounty
Flat **2–5 USDT per activated referral**, funded from the deploy fee (3 USDT)
plus treasury top-up, capped per epoch (e.g. 500 activations / referrer /
month). Simple to explain, fast feedback loop, bounded downside.

### b) Penalty-fee revenue share
**10–20% of penalty fees** generated by your referred users, for **12 months**
from each user's signup. Tiered by active referral count:

| Tier | Activated referrals | Penalty revshare |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 1–9 | 10% |
| Silver | 10–99 | 15% |
| Gold | 100–999 | 20% + co-marketing |
| Whale | 1000+ | negotiated + co-branded vaults |

Note the honest tension: penalties come from users breaking their own savings
discipline — we should market this as "aligned with usage," not "aligned with
failure," and consider weighting toward (a)/(d) in public messaging.

### c) Deploy-fee kickback
**1 of the 3 USDT** deploy fee routed to the referrer when a referred user
deploys their permanent deposit address. Zero new cost (fee split, not fee
add), trivially attributable.

### d) Retroactive points / airdrop weighting ("what to promise today")
Since future benefits are undecided, the strongest immediate offer is a
**provable, timestamped on-chain claim on whatever comes later**:

```
points(referrer) = Σ over activated referrals of
                   referred_TVL × retention_time × decay(churn)
```

- Snapshot-based (e.g. monthly), computed off-chain from events, published
  as a merkle root.
- Weighted by referred **TVL × time held**, not headcount — an influencer
  bringing 50 real savers beats one bringing 5000 empty wallets.
- Early-referrer multiplier (e.g. 2× for the first 6 months) rewards
  influencers who take the early risk — this is the pitch: *"your audience is
  recorded on-chain from day one; any future token/points program reads that
  record."*

### e) Two-sided sweetener
Referred users get their **deposit-address deploy fee waived or halved**.
Gives influencers a concrete benefit to advertise ("sign up with my link, save
the fee") — conversion on the referee side is usually the binding constraint.

## 5. Anti-abuse

- **Self-referral**: blocked on-chain (`Cannot refer yourself`) and filtered
  client-side before the transaction.
- **Sybil farming**: activation threshold (deposit + 30-day retention) makes
  farmed wallets capital- and time-expensive; per-referrer epoch caps bound
  worst-case exposure; points decay on churned users claws back grinders.
- **Deposit cycling** (deposit → wait → withdraw → re-deposit elsewhere):
  retention weighting uses *net* TVL over time, so cycling earns ~nothing.
- **Referrer-of-referrer loops**: single-level program only; no multi-level
  payouts (also keeps it clear of MLM classification).
- **Last-click hijacking**: the frontend is first-capture-wins per browser
  until commit, and the record is immutable after commit.
- **Payout safety**: claims are pull-based from a dedicated rewards pot, never
  from user funds or module custody; the pot going empty degrades to "accrue
  now, claim later," never to touching custody.

## 6. Future on-chain work

- **ReferralModule v2**: accrual accounting (`accrued[referrer]`), `claim()`,
  epoch snapshots, per-source revshare config (bps of penalties / deploy fees).
- **Fee hooks**: ProxyDeploymentModule and VaultSystemModule route the
  referrer's share at fee-collection time (lookup via `getReferrer`).
- **Vanity codes**: on-chain `code → address` registry so links can be
  `?ref=CRYPTOJOE` instead of a raw address.
- **Solana parity**: referrer field captured at vault/deposit-proxy creation,
  mirroring the EVM record.

## 7. Open questions

- Reward denomination: USDT vs. future token (or points convertible later)?
- Epoch length and caps for bounties (weekly vs. monthly)?
- Legal/geo review: revenue-share to influencers may be regulated promotion in
  some jurisdictions (e.g. UK FCA financial-promotions regime, US state MTL
  angles) — needs counsel before public launch.
- Do we publicly display a referrer leaderboard (growth lever) or keep
  referral stats private to each referrer (privacy-first)?
